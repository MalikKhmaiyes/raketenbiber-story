One day I was using my staubsauger to clean up after my German shephard.
She had eaten my gebaeck which I was having for breakfast!
I tried to get it back and tripped over the Staubsauger, turning it on.
I fell back into the music player and Rammstein started blasting over the speaker.
I almost choked on my broetchen.

Startled by the music, my Hund chased the Staubsauger into a Biergarten, where a waiter tried to stop it with a giant Broetchen.
